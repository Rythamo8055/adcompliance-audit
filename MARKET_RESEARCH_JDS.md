## Executive Summary: What Problem Are Brands Actually Hiring For?

Across both Indian high-growth D2C brands ($1M–$50M ARR) and US/Global performance marketing agencies managing 400+ brands, there is an identical, massive operational bottleneck:

1. **High Volume of Creator Deliverables:** Brands contract 50 to 500+ creators and UGC freelancers every month across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
2. **The Deliverables Review Chokepoint:** Every video must be watched multiple times by human coordinators to inspect:
   - **Contractual Must-Haves:** Did they say the exact promo code? Is the discount code visible on-screen? Did they show the physical product in the first 3 seconds (hook)?
   - **Regulatory & Medical Compliance:** ASCI (India) or FTC (US) #ad/#sponsored disclosure placed properly? No forbidden medical cure claims (e.g., in hair loss or skincare)?
   - **Format & Platform Specs:** 9:16 vertical ratio, 1080p, no audio clipping, no critical text covered by TikTok/Reels UI buttons (caption area, like/comment column).
3. **Payment Release Gating:** The operations team cannot release creator payments or payout invoices until the deliverable is validated and marked compliant.
4. **Creator Friction & Fatigue:** When human coordinators reject content, they often write rude or vague feedback ("fix the video"), prompting creator arguments, ghosting, or delayed campaign launches.

## 4. Why Has Nobody Done This? Why Do Generic Tools Fail?

1. **Why Modash / Aspire / Grin Haven't Solved This:**
   - They are **discovery and CRM platforms** (sourcing, emailing, contracting, tracking links).
   - Once a creator sends an `.mp4` file or Google Drive link, the CRM just shows a download link. A human still has to watch it.
2. **Why Generic Video AI Tools Fail:**
   - Tools like CapCut or generic video transcribers do not know **D2C performance marketing rules** (safe zones, hook timing, promo code matching, ASCI/FTC laws).
3. **Why Video QA Breaks When Built Naively:**
   - Trying to pass raw 1080p video into LLMs naively hits massive token costs or timeouts.
   - Using small local vision models (e.g., Qwen Vision) fails because of strict 3-image limits or lack of audio understanding.
   - Relying on LLMs for binary specs (e.g., "is this 9:16?") produces hallucinations.
4. **Our Defensible Edge:**
   - **Hybrid Architecture:** Deterministic FFmpeg probes for binary specs + Google Gemini Native Multimodal Video for semantic hook & safe-zone intelligence.
   - **Cost Advantage:** Leveraging Flash-Lite 1M context windows + multi-key rotation yields $0.00 marginal cost for over 3,000 video audits/day.
